# Practice questions: how the questions inside study material are written, placed and answered

Question: how are the questions inside study material written, placed and answered, so that answering them makes the learner learn more, and what does the evidence say about their kind, format, wording and feedback?

One reviewer searched on 2026-10-29, by the [shared method](../README.md#method).

- It covers published research and public practice: testing-effect research, item writing, feedback research, question generation, open textbooks, quiz tools and lesson templates.
- It holds 82 formal sources (73 new, 3 of them by title only) and 25 grey sources (23 new: 8 from the web, 15 Q/A threads). One new grey source, NBME 2026, is an example only: the second rater scored it 6.5, read by its landing page.
- Whether testing works at all is held in the core and linked, not reviewed again.
- A number read only in an abstract or summary is marked "(secondary)".

## Named baseline

- To beat: **B1**, a self-test of short-answer recall questions with the correct answer shown after each attempt.
- Practice control: **B0**, end-of-chapter review questions as a textbook prints them (Spielman 2020).
- Comparison: delayed test on practised, new inference and distractor-linked questions, with peeking logged ([baseline](baseline.md)).
